# Dragon Arena v0.8 gameplay and UI interaction acceptance
Actual offline-only rules: fixed 60 Hz tick; 10 weighted food families; respawning food target 165; 180 world units; 8 initial to 220 maximum body nodes (one node per 3 mass). Base speed 6.25 u/s, boost 9.75 u/s, max turning 4.5 rad/s. Head-head collisions resolve by base-adjusted mass ratio 1.24 (near equals both lose); shields block collisions, not outer boundary. Death emits collected mass drops (65%) or four-item initial bounty. Live local bots use 24 cosmetic identities, different behavior and respawn after 3.5 s; ranks derive from local match score and do not represent real users.
Controls: joystick + boost multitouch, boost release on blur/cancel, speed 4 s, magnet 7 s, shield 10 s (simulation tick based). Local energy max 20, +1 every 15 minutes; Quick Play 1 energy, Practice free. Cosmetic purchases do not boost competitive stats. Real money, ads, online friend play, cloud save, and global ranks are not implemented; never simulate them as live.
Character appearances: after unpack, run tools/shape_variants_v8.py to append different antlers, ears, fins, armor, plume and tail geometry by family; 24 updated physical GLB meshes.
Release blocking gates: Android phone touch, FPS, multitouch, pause/resume, persistence, screenshot-level 3D visual fidelity, server authority for online services. Draft PR stays unmerged until accepted.
